Privacy Policy

File Manager AI+

Effective date: 7 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how File Manager AI+ ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Summary

  • Your files, photos, videos, and documents are managed locally on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • When you use AI features, the specific content or metadata you choose to analyze may be sent securely to our processing service to generate insights.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase for app integrity and performance.
  • Files you add to the Secure Vault stay on your device and are protected by your PIN or biometric lock.

2. Information We Access and Collect

a. Files and media on your device

To work as a file manager, the App needs access to the files, photos, videos, audio, and documents stored on your device and SD card. This access is used only to let you browse, organize, copy, move, rename, delete, share, analyze, and protect your files. This content is processed on your device and is not uploaded to us, except for the AI features described in Section 3.

b. Information processed for AI features

When you actively use an AI-powered feature (for example, smart storage insights or file analysis), the relevant file content or metadata you select is transmitted over a secure connection to our backend, which uses Google's Gemini API to generate results. This data is used only to provide the requested feature and is not used to build advertising profiles.

c. Automatically collected data

Our third-party providers (see Section 5) may automatically collect certain technical information, such as:

Data typePurpose

Approximate location (derived from IP address)Ad relevance, fraud prevention

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide core file management and storage analysis features.
  • To generate AI-powered insights you request.
  • To secure files you place in the Vault using your PIN/biometric lock.
  • To display and measure advertisements.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve app performance.

6. Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers listed above, strictly to operate the App, or where required by law.

7. Data Retention

Files remain on your device until you delete them. Data processed for AI features is retained only as long as needed to deliver the feature. Advertising and diagnostic data is retained by the respective providers per their policies.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections for network requests and on-device protection for the Vault. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to opt out of personalized ads via your device's Ads settings. To exercise these rights, contact us below.
